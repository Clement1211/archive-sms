<section class="page-hero"><p class="eyebrow">PRIVACY</p><h1>Your messages stay on your device.</h1><p class="lead">The reader processes your backup in your browser. Message contents are not sent to SMS Archive.</p></section><article class="article"><h2>File and archive</h2><p>Your XML file is read by the browser. Imported conversations are stored in that browser’s IndexedDB on your device. Storage limits depend on your browser and device.</p><h2>Website connection</h2><p>Like any website, the hosting provider may process technical connection data under its own policy. SMS Archive does not receive the contents of your imported file.</p><h2>Deletion</h2><p>Use the reader’s Delete button to remove the archive stored on this device. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings also removes that copy.</p><h2>Contact</h2><p>For questions about this policy, visit <a href="{{ '/en/contact/' | relative_url }}">Contact</a>.</p></article>
