<section class="page-hero"><p class="eyebrow">IPHONE GUIDE</p><h1>Read an SMS backup on iPhone</h1><p class="lead">Transfer the XML file to your iPhone, then open it in the reader with Safari.</p></section><article class="article"><h2>1. Transfer the XML file</h2><p>Send the backup from your computer or Android phone to iPhone using AirDrop, iCloud Drive, or another transfer method. Make sure it appears in the Files app.</p><h2>2. Open SMS Archive in Safari</h2><p>Visit SMS Archive and tap <a href="{{ '/app/' | relative_url }}">Open the reader</a>. Safari is recommended for importing and adding the app to your Home Screen.</p><h2>3. Import the backup</h2><p>In the app, tap Import, choose the XML file in Files, and let the import finish. Keep Safari open during the import.</p><h2>4. Browse conversations</h2><p>Search for a contact or message. Contact names and MMS media appear when they are included in the export.</p><h2>Add the app to your Home Screen</h2><p>In Safari, open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. The icon opens the reader directly.</p><p>Import time and available storage vary with your device and backup size. Keep the original XML file.</p></article>
