<section class="page-hero"><p class="eyebrow">SMS &amp; MMS</p><h1>Contact names and MMS attachments</h1><p class="lead">What appears in SMS Archive depends on the information included in the original XML export.</p></section><article class="article"><h2>Contact names</h2><p>When the export includes the name saved for a contact, the reader displays it in the list and conversation. If the XML has no name, the address or phone number is used instead.</p><h2>Images and MMS attachments</h2><p>Media included in an MMS backup appears in the conversation when it is present in the XML file.</p><h2>Why information may be missing</h2><p>The export may not contain a name or attachment, for example if that information was unavailable when the backup was created. The reader can only show what is in the file.</p><p><a class="button primary" href="{{ '/app/' | relative_url }}">Open the reader</a></p></article>
